Apple Health and pulse

How do I connect Apple Health?

In VitalHer, go to You, then Heart rate, then Connect, and tap Allow. On Apple’s screen, tap Turn On All, then Allow. You can change this later in the iPhone Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then VitalHer.

Why does my pulse show “Waiting for watch”?

An Apple Watch sends heart rate to your iPhone every few minutes, unless a workout is running on the watch. For live pulse, start a Traditional Strength Training workout on your Apple Watch when you start a training in VitalHer, and end it when you finish.

Can I use a Garmin watch?

Yes, in two ways. For live pulse during training, turn on Broadcast Heart Rate on the watch (on most models: hold UP or open the controls menu). Then in VitalHer, go to You, then Heart rate, and tap Connect next to Live over Bluetooth. Turn on Broadcast Heart Rate before each training.

For resting pulse and other trends, turn on syncing with Apple Health in the Garmin Connect app. That data arrives when the watch syncs, so it isn’t live.

Can I use a chest strap?

Yes. Polar, Wahoo, Garmin HRM and other Bluetooth heart rate straps work. Put the strap on, wet the pads, then in VitalHer go to You, then Heart rate, and tap Connect next to Live over Bluetooth. A chest strap is the most accurate choice for strength training.

Why is my VO₂ max empty?

Apple Watch estimates VO₂ max during outdoor walks, runs or hikes of at least 20 minutes. It appears in VitalHer once Apple Health has a value.

I take medication that lowers my heart rate

Go to You, then Heart rate, and turn on I take medication that lowers my heart rate. VitalHer then guides you by breathing and effort instead of pulse zones.

Training

How do I change my training days?

Go to You, then Training days, and pick 2 to 6 days. To move a single training this week, tap the day in the week strip on the Today tab.

What is a light day?

When you train two days in a row, the second training becomes lighter: two sets per exercise at the same weights. It helps you recover, especially when sleep and energy are up and down.

An exercise hurts. What should I do?

Stop the exercise. Tap Swap exercise for a joint-friendly version, or tell VitalHer which joint is sore in the check-in before your training. If pain lasts more than a few days, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. VitalHer has no accounts or servers. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In VitalHer, go to You and tap Reset the app twice. Or delete the app from your iPhone.

I got a new iPhone. Where is my history?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. VitalHer’s data is included in those backups.

Subscription

How does the free trial work?

VitalHer Premium starts with 14 days free. You can look around and build your plan without it, but trainings need VitalHer Premium. Choose yearly or monthly when you start the trial. Apple charges your Apple ID when the trial ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app.

I already subscribe. Why does VitalHer ask me again?

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, go to You, then VitalHer Premium, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then VitalHer, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

VitalHer is a training guide, not medical advice. If you have heart problems, high blood pressure or recent surgery, check with your doctor before you start. In an emergency, call 113 in Norway or your local emergency number.
