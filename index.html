Why strength, why now

From your late 30s, muscle and bone are lost faster. Menopause speeds it up.

Keep your muscle

Lifting weights two to three times a week is one of the best-studied ways to hold on to muscle and strength as you get older.

Protect your bones

Bones respond to load. Progressive strength training and short, safe impacts give them a reason to stay strong.

Stay steady

Stronger legs and better balance help you move with confidence, at home, at work and on the trail.

Adapts to you

Your training changes with how you feel

Before each training, a quick check-in asks how you are. Today’s session adjusts to match.

  • Cooler sessions on hot-flush days, calmer ones when you’re stressed or low
  • A lighter day after a bad night, an extra set when you feel strong
  • Joint-friendly swaps for knees, shoulders and back, and a spine-safe mode
  • Tell the coach how you feel in your own words, and it suggests a session

A clear plan

Every exercise, shown step by step

Full-body training days of 20 to 60 minutes, at the gym or at home with dumbbells. Never lifted before? You start where you are.

  • Moving demos and short, plain cues for every exercise
  • Weights that grow with you, with a lighter week every six weeks
  • A new block of exercises every six weeks, so training stays fresh
  • Two to six days a week, on the days that suit you

Symptom diary

See your patterns. Bring a report to your doctor.

Log hot flushes, sleep, mood, anxiety, tiredness, brain fog, aches and headaches in about 30 seconds a day.

  • Trends over 4, 8 or 12 weeks, next to your periods and training days
  • Patterns from your own log, such as how you sleep after training days
  • A clear one- or two-page PDF to share with your doctor

Questions

Who is VitalHer for?

Women from 35 in perimenopause, menopause or after, whether you’re new to weights or coming back after a break.

Do I need a gym?

No. Choose gym or home when you set up. At home, a set of dumbbells is enough, and VitalHer picks exercises that work with them.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. VitalHer works on its own. With Apple Health and a watch, it can use your sleep, resting heart rate and more to guide each session.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 15 or later. The on-device AI coach needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Without it, VitalHer uses its own built-in texts.

Is this medical advice?

No. VitalHer gives general training guidance and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor before you start a new exercise programme, especially if you have a health condition.
