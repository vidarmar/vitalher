Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your iPhone

Version 1.1 · Draft, 4 November 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. VitalHer doesn’t know who you are.
  • Your training log and settings stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • Apple Health data is read on your iPhone and never leaves it. It isn’t uploaded, shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The app has no servers of its own.

1. Who we are

VitalHer: Strength through menopause (“VitalHer”, “the app”) is made by Marisson Aviation Consulting, a sole proprietorship (enkeltpersonforetak) registered in Norway, organisation number 917 105 375 MVA, Maristien 7B, 4638 Kristiansand, Norway (“we”, “us”). Contact: vidar@maravconsult.com.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. It applies to the iPhone app and to this website.

2. Information stored on your iPhone

To work, VitalHer stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage:

  • Your settings: age, joints to protect, training days, session length, equipment, weight unit, text size and colours.
  • Your training log: which trainings you completed, the weights and repetitions you logged, and how hard a training felt.
  • When you accepted the User Agreement, and which version.

We don’t receive or see any of this. If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, your iPhone includes the app’s data in that backup under Apple’s terms, so you keep your history when you change phones.

3. Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, VitalHer asks your permission to read:

  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability
  • VO₂ max
  • Walking steadiness
  • Workouts

VitalHer uses this data only on your iPhone to show your pulse during training, guide your rest between sets, and show your progress. VitalHer:

  • does not write anything to Apple Health,
  • does not send Apple Health data to us or to anyone else,
  • does not use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining,
  • does not store Apple Health data in iCloud or on any server.

Connecting is optional. You can change or withdraw access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then VitalHer.

3a. Bluetooth heart rate sensors

If you connect a heart rate strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate (for example a Garmin with Broadcast Heart Rate turned on), VitalHer uses Bluetooth to receive your pulse during training. The readings are used only on your iPhone, in the same way as Apple Health data, and are never uploaded or shared. VitalHer remembers the name of the sensor you chose so it can reconnect. You can forget the sensor in the app, under You, then Heart rate, or turn off Bluetooth access in Settings, then VitalHer.

4. Purchases

If VitalHer offers a subscription or other purchase, it is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, card details or Apple ID. Apple provides us with general sales reports that do not identify you. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.

5. Who else is involved

  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight and may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example crash reports if you have turned on sharing with app developers. If you test the app in TestFlight and send feedback, Apple passes that feedback, and any screenshot you attach, to us.
  • GitHub hosts this website. GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses when you visit, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The app itself does not contact GitHub.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and its typefaces are built in, so it makes no requests to third-party services.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Because we don’t collect or receive your personal data, we don’t process it on our side. The data described in sections 2 and 3 is processed on your own device, under your control.

You can delete all of VitalHer’s data at any time:

  • In the app, go to You and tap Reset the app.
  • Or delete the app from your iPhone.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete the correspondence when it’s no longer needed. You have the right to access, correct and delete that information. If you believe we have handled your personal data incorrectly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), or the authority where you live.

7. Age

VitalHer is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly allow use by children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, for example by adding an optional online feature, we will update this policy, change the version and date above, and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
